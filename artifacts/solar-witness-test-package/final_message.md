### 4. Witness-Test Scope, Roles, and Prerequisites for the Three Not-Carried Arrays (`WP #47`, `WP #48`, `WP #49`)

For each of the three non-carried arrays (**Darnwick Elementary School Array**, **Holcott Public Library Array**, and **Lockmere Middle School Array**):

1. **Tests to Be Performed and Their Basis (`ENG-PRC-014 Rev 3 §4.2`; `IEEE 1547.1`; `IEEE 1547-2018`; `DIS-01 Rev 4 §5 Table 5-1 Category 2 & §6`; `DPS-07 Rev 2 §4 & §6.2`)**:
   * **Protective-Function Trip Tests & Reconnect Timing**: Functional pickup magnitude and clearing-time testing of undervoltage (`ANSI 27`), overvoltage (`ANSI 59`), underfrequency (`ANSI 81U`), and overfrequency (`ANSI 81O`) elements, synchronism check (`ANSI 25`), directional power (`ANSI 32`), feeder overcurrent coordination (`ANSI 50/51, 50N/51N`), DER breaker local/remote auxiliary trip, utility POI recloser reclose-block interlock (where applicable, such as Lockmere Middle School), and return-to-service reconnect delay timing against the approved site settings sheet and coordination study.
   * **Anti-Islanding Function Test**: Open-utility / loss-of-utility-source unintentional islanding test per `IEEE 1547.1` verifying cessation of energization and trip within `≤ 2.0 s` on the actually installed inverter (`Arvexa PV-750` at Darnwick Elementary; `Selvix SV-720` at Holcott Public Library and Lockmere Middle School).
   * **Voltage and Frequency Ride-Through (VRT/FRT) Verifications**: Verification of programmed `IEEE 1547-2018` Category II (and Region III at Lockmere Middle School) voltage and frequency ride-through profiles and reactive power / voltage support settings against the approved settings sheet.

2. **Who Performs, Schedules, and Witnesses the Tests (`ENG-PRC-014 Rev 3 §4.2 & §4.3`)**:
   * **Performed by**: The **customer** (`Darnwick Community School District`, `Town of Holcott`, `Lockmere Community School District`) or the customer's **commissioning agent / EPC of record** (`M. Halbrook, P.E.`, Badge `AFE-1042`, and/or `K. Ondras`, Badge `AFE-2231`, Field Services), who supplies the written test plan, calibrated test instrumentation (with instrument identifiers), and qualified personnel to operate the equipment.
   * **Scheduled by**: The **DER Program Commissioning Coordinator**, targeting the earliest mutually available date consistent with construction-complete status and witness engineer availability.
   * **Witnessed & Dispositioned by**: An **Alleniom Utilities Witness Engineer** (**Standards Engineering — Evan Solberg** or designated Alleniom witness engineer), who observes the test on site, records instrument identifiers and measured values, and signs and dates the pass/fail witness-test record per `ENG-PRC-014 Rev 3 §4.2–4.3`.

3. **What Has to Be on File First (`ENG-PRC-014 Rev 3 §4.1–4.3`; `DIS-01 Rev 4 §6–7`; `TCS-02 §5`)**:
   * **Prior to Conducting the On-Site Witness Test**:
     * Completed construction, protection settings, grounding, and labeling inspection sign-offs (Sections 1–4 / A–D Pass; plus completion of the pending Field Services Supervisor countersignature on Page 3 of `CX-HOL-LIB-01` for Holcott Public Library).
     * Updated commissioning checklist re-marked from the batch copy-forward `"waiver eligible"` to **`"witness test required"`** referencing `CEL-2025-Q2` (`04/30/2025`).
     * **Additional Prerequisite for Lockmere Middle School Array (`COMM-LKM-MS-2025-06`, `PXA-2024-0793`)**: Amended/corrected commissioning checklist, updated as-built single-line diagram / equipment schedule (and ISA equipment change-order documentation under `DIS-01 Rev 4 §7` for same-nameplate `720 kW` model substitution), and updated protection settings & anti-islanding coordination record replacing the superseded `4 × Torvane Electric TE-180K` entries (`S/N TE180K-24-11482 / -11487 / -11491 / -11502`, FW `4.11.02`) with the **actually installed `Selvix SV-720`** serial number, firmware version, and protection parameters.
     * Approved site-specific protection settings sheet, single-line diagram, and protection coordination study.
     * Customer / commissioning agent written **witness test plan**, **calibrated instrument identifiers and calibration certificates**, and scheduling/test-plan correspondence (`ENG-PRC-014 Rev 3 §4.2–4.3`).
     * Manufacturer's certificate of conformance, factory test report, or technical documentation for the installed inverter (`Arvexa PV-750` or `Selvix SV-720`).
   * **Prior to Permission to Operate (PTO) Issuance by the SOC**:
     * Complete single commissioning record required by `ENG-PRC-014 Rev 3 §4.3`, including the **Alleniom witness engineer's signed and dated witness-test record** (with instrument identifiers, measured values, and pass/fail dispositions per test point).
     * **Live RTU-to-SCADA telemetry confirmation by the SOC Shift Supervisor (`TCS-02 §3.6 & §5`, `DIS-01 Rev 4 §6`)**: Already completed and live since `06/17/2025` for **Lockmere Middle School Array** (`RTU-LKM-171-04`, SCADA ticket `TKT-25-04188`), but still **Pending** utility-owned RTU installation, SCADA point verification, communications path testing, and SOC live-telemetry confirmation for **Darnwick Elementary School Array** (`Section 5.1–5.4 Pending`) and **Holcott Public Library Array** (`Section E-1, E-2, E-4 Pending`).
