### Summary of Verified Findings & Numerical Working

1. **Subsurface Profile, In-Situ Stress State, and Laboratory Consolidation Parameters (`KGI-2024-0417 Rev.2`, `Boring_Logs_and_GW_Monitoring_B01-B09.pdf`, `Geotech_Index_Test_Data.xlsx`, `VMT-2025-0109-CON`):**
   - Beneath Block C (grade $\text{El. } 750.0\text{ ft}$; Boring B-09 at $\text{El. } 749.9\text{ ft}$), Stratum 1 medium-dense SP-SM sand ($\gamma_1 = 118\text{ pcf}$, design water table $\text{El. } 742.0\text{ ft}$) extends to $\text{El. } 738.0\text{ ft}$, underlain by Stratum 2 compressible lean clay aquitard (USCS CL, $\text{El. } 738.0\text{ ft}$ to $\text{El. } 720.0\text{ ft}$, thickness $H = 18.0\text{ ft} = 216\text{ in}$, $\gamma_2 = 120\text{ pcf}$) and Stratum 3 dense SW-GW sand and gravel below $\text{El. } 720.0\text{ ft}$ ($\gamma_3 = 130\text{ pcf}$, confined piezometric head $\text{El. } 738.0\text{ ft}$, double-drained boundaries with $H_{\text{dr}} = 9.0\text{ ft}$).
   - ASTM D2435 oedometer testing (`VMT-2025-0109-CON`, Talia Brennard, PE, `01/09/2025`) on undisturbed Shelby-tube samples B-06 U3 ($\text{El. } 729.5\text{ ft}$, depth $10.0\text{–}12.5\text{ ft}$) and B-08 U2 ($\text{El. } 728.7\text{ ft}$, depth $7.5\text{–}10.0\text{ ft}$) established: $C_c = 0.28$, $C_r = 0.045$, $e_0 = 0.92$, in-situ vertical effective stress $\sigma'_0 = 1,850\text{ psf}$ at mid-depth ($\text{El. } 729.0\text{ ft}$), Casagrande preconsolidation pressure $\sigma'_p = 1,900\text{ psf}$ ($\text{OCR} = 1.03$, margin $\sigma'_p - \sigma'_0 = 50\text{ psf}$), and virgin coefficient of consolidation $c_v = 4.5 \times 10^{-4}\text{ cm}^2/\text{s} \approx 0.042\text{ ft}^2/\text{day}$.

2. **Critical Omission in Calculation `CB-GEO-007` (Rev.0, `09/30/2025`):**
   - On Sheet 2 of `CB-GEO-007`, Calloway Braddock (Gregory Nash, PE) establishes Block C spread-footing service loads (F-C1 through F-C12, $8.5\text{ ft} \times 8.5\text{ ft}$ to $10.5\text{ ft} \times 10.5\text{ ft}$, $q_{\text{net}} = 3,450\text{ to }3,730\text{ psf}$) and a combined Boussinesq/Newmark vertical stress increase at mid-clay ($\text{El. } 729.0\text{ ft}$) of $\Delta\sigma = 900\text{ psf}$.
   - On Sheet 3, the EOR computes only immediate elastic settlement by the Schmertmann method ($S_i = 0.70\text{ to }0.90\text{ in}$, maximum $0.90\text{ in}$ at F-C6, $0.82\text{ in}$ at F-C7, maximum differential $\Delta S_i = 0.20\text{ in}$), which already consumes $90\%$ of the $1.0\text{ in}$ total settlement limit.
   - On **Sheet 4 (Consolidation)**, every consolidation entry ($\sigma'_0, \Delta\sigma, \sigma'_p, H, C_c, e_0, \text{Result}$) is left blank (`—`) with the note: *"Not computed. Serviceability taken from the immediate settlement result on Sheet 3,"* while Sheet 5 merely lists the Vantree parameters *"for the record and to close out the transmittal."* This directly violates `KGI-2024-0417 Rev.2` Section 1 (p. 6) and Section 9.1 (pp. 16–17). In addition, `CB-GEO-007` Sheet 2 uses a footing base elevation of $\text{El. } 740.5\text{ ft}$ ($2.5\text{ ft}$ above top of clay), whereas Structural Drawing `S-201 Rev.4` Detail 3/S-201 specifies $\text{El. } 746.0\text{ ft}$.

3. **Independent Computation of Primary Consolidation ($S_c$), Total ($S_{\text{total}}$), and Differential Settlement:**
   - With $H / (1 + e_0) = 18.0\text{ ft} / 1.92 = 9.375\text{ ft} = 112.50\text{ in}$, $\sigma'_0 = 1,850\text{ psf}$, $\sigma'_p = 1,900\text{ psf}$, and $\sigma'_f = \sigma'_0 + \Delta\sigma = 2,750\text{ psf}$:
     - **Exact Two-Branch (Recompression + Virgin) Consolidation Settlement:**
       $$S_c = 112.50\text{ in} \times \left[0.045 \log_{10}\!\left(\frac{1,900}{1,850}\right) + 0.28 \log_{10}\!\left(\frac{2,750}{1,900}\right)\right] = 0.0586\text{ in} + 5.0582\text{ in} = \mathbf{5.12\text{ in}}\text{ (}0.426\text{ ft)}$$
     - **Single-Branch Normally-Consolidated Virgin Equation (`KGI-2024-0417 Rev.2` Section 8.1, p. 14):**
       $$S_c = 112.50\text{ in} \times 0.28 \log_{10}\!\left(\frac{2,750}{1,850}\right) = 31.50\text{ in} \times 0.172161 = \mathbf{5.42\text{ in}}\text{ (}0.452\text{ ft)}$$
     - **Combined Total Settlement ($S_{\text{total}} = S_i + S_c$ at F-C6):**
       $$S_{\text{total}} = 0.90\text{ in} + 5.12\text{ in} = \mathbf{6.02\text{ in}}\text{ (bilinear)} \quad \text{or} \quad 0.90\text{ in} + 5.42\text{ in} = \mathbf{6.32\text{ in}}\text{ (single-branch NC)}$$
     - **Exceedance of Project Limits:** Primary consolidation settlement alone ($5.12\text{ to }5.42\text{ in}$) exceeds the **$1.0\text{ in}$ maximum total settlement tolerance** by more than **$5\times$** ($512\%\text{–}542\%$), and combined total settlement ($6.02\text{ to }6.32\text{ in}$) exceeds the limit by over $5.0\text{ in}$. Long-term differential settlement between interior footings (F-C6/F-C7) and edge/corner footings reaches **$1.36\text{ to }2.50\text{ in}$** ($272\%\text{–}500\%$ of the **$0.5\text{ in}$ maximum differential settlement limit**, violating the $1/500$ angular distortion limit).

4. **Time-Rate of Consolidation and Correlation with Field Monitoring (`Settlement_Monitoring_BlockC.xlsx`):**
   - For double drainage ($H_{\text{dr}} = 9.0\text{ ft}$, $H_{\text{dr}}^2 = 81.0\text{ ft}^2$) and $c_v = 0.042\text{ ft}^2/\text{day}$, $t_{50} = 380\text{ days}$ ($1.04\text{ yr}$) and $t_{90} = 1,635\text{ days}$ ($4.48\text{ yr}$).
   - Across the 15 biweekly survey rounds (`12/12/2025` to `06/26/2026`, $196\text{ days}$ elapsed), cumulative settlement has already reached **$-0.61\text{ in}$** at interior point **MP-7** (FTG-C7) and **$-0.58\text{ in}$** at **MP-6** (FTG-C6) vs. **$-0.31\text{ in}$** at corner **MP-12** (FTG-C12), with measured differential settlement already at **$0.23\text{ in}$** between adjacent columns (MP-7 vs. MP-3 and MP-8 vs. MP-12) and **$0.30\text{ in}$** across the footprint.
