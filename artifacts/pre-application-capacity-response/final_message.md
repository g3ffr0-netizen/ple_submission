#### B. Determination 2 (Request Item 2): Known Conditions, Restrictions, Upgrade Indications & Sizing Guidance
- **Call Made**: **RESTRICTIONS & UPGRADE INDICATIONS IDENTIFIED; RESIZING CAP OF `≤ 0.8 MW` (`800 kW`) ADVISED TO FIT CURRENT WORKING HEADROOM.**
- **Basis & Advisories to Requester**:
  1. **Impact of Filing at `3.0 MW-AC` (`3.9 MW-DC`)**: Exceeds the `0.8 MW` remaining working headroom by **`2.2 MW` (`2,200 kW`)**. Classified as **Medium-Scale DER (`1 MW – 5 MW`)** under APUC Interconnection Tariff Vol. III, `§12.2(b)` and `DIS-01 Rev 4 §3 Table 3-1`. Filing at `3.0 MW-AC` will fail initial technical screening and Supplemental Review Procedure `SRP-3 Rev 3 §3 Screen A` & `§5(c)` (aggregate DER penetration), require a full **System Impact Study (`SIS`)** under the DER Planning Group (`Ivo Grantham`), and likely trigger substantial applicant-funded thermal, voltage, and/or protection system upgrades.
  2. **Resizing Option (`≤ 0.8 MW` / `800 kW`)**: Consistent with Northline's stated intent to sit at or below feeder headroom, sizing within current working capacity on `ALN-108` requires reducing the requested AC interconnection capacity to **`≤ 0.8 MW` (`800 kW`)** (`Commercial Small-Scale DER`, `500 kW – 1 MW` under `Tariff §12.2(a)` and `DIS-01 Rev 4 Table 3-1 & Table 5-1 Category 2`).
  3. **Controlled-Capacity Recognition (`DIS-01 Rev 4 §3.1 & §3.6`)**: Aggregate inverter AC nameplate governs unless Northline submits **both** (1) a valid `UL 1741` / `UL 3141` Power Control System (`PCS`) certification (`CEL-2025-Q2`) and (2) an approved site-specific **Control Narrative** at application intake.
  4. **Queue Position & Prohibition on Post-Filing / Post-ISA Capacity Amendments (`Tariff §12.3, §12.6, §12.7`; `DIS-01 Rev 4 §7`; `POL-QM-05 Rev 3 §2.4`)**: A pre-application request does not establish a queue position or reserve headroom (`Tariff §12.3`). Formal applications are processed in queue order behind `PXA-2023-0894`, `PXA-2024-0091`, `PXA-2024-0231`, and `PXA-2025-0044` (`Tariff §12.7`), and material modifications to capacity or POI require withdrawal and re-application with a new queue date (`Tariff §12.6`, `DIS-01 Rev 4 §7`, `POL-QM-05 Rev 3 §2.4`).
  5. **Telemetry, POI Protection & Equipment Lead-Time Advisory (`TCS-02 §5`, `DPS-07 Rev 2 §4.2`, `DIS-01 Rev 4 §5 Table 5-1`, `OG-17`, `SCADA-EQP-2025-06`)**: All in-scope projects (`≥ 500 kW`) require utility-owned RTU and live SOC SCADA telemetry prior to PTO (`TCS-02 §5`), and `Category 3` (`1 MW – 5 MW`) export projects also require a utility-owned POI recloser (`DPS-07 Rev 2 §4.2`). Current supplier lead times for utility RTUs and POI reclosers are approximately **8 months** (`SCADA-EQP-2025-06`), with deployment prioritized under `OG-17`.
  6. **Alternative Elderpine `13.2 kV` Pure-Generation Feeders (`ALN-097` and `ALN-103`)**: Adjacent Elderpine `13.2 kV` pure-generation feeders **`ALN-097`** (**`6.1 MW`** published available on `HCM-2024-Q2`) and **`ALN-103`** (**`5.5 MW`** published available on `HCM-2024-Q2`) have **zero (`0`)** active or on-hold queue entries as of `07/14/2025` and can be evaluated during the pre-application dialogue if Northline requires a full `3.0 MW` POI.
