#### A. Governing Acceptance Basis & Scope Exclusions
- **Governing Specification Basis**: **Specification 31 20 00 (Earth Moving, Rev. 3, `03/06/2026`) Article 3.4.B** (*Compaction and Moisture Criteria — Structural Fill*), **Article 3.4.C.3** (*Non-Averaging Rule*), and **Article 3.4.D** (*Interpretation*).
- **Explicit Scope Exclusions**:
  - *ASTM D698 Standard Proctor is for historical borrow characterization only and is excluded as an acceptance basis for Package A structural fill per Article 1.2.A.1.*
  - *Averaging isolated failing field density or moisture tests with adjacent passing tests is prohibited per Article 3.4.C.3.*
- **Laboratory Compaction Standard**: **ASTM D1557-12(2021) Method C** (Modified Proctor, $56,000\text{ ft-lbf/ft}^3$ compactive effort) per **Vantree Materials Testing Report No. `VMT-25-0918-P04`** (dated `09/18/2025` [`2025-09-18`], Sample ID **`LWP-PA-SF-2025-01`**, Larchwood Pit — Package A Structural Fill, USCS `SM / SW-SM`, $9.4\%$ passing No. 200 sieve, Non-Plastic, $G_s = 2.68$):

  - **Optimum Moisture Content ($w_{\text{opt}}$)**: **$9.2\%$**

#### A. Evaluation of Field Testing Execution and Reporting (`SF-26-0708`)
1. **Conforming Aspects of Field Execution**:

### 4. Sufficiency of What Was Done and Recorded Under Specification 31 20 00

What was done and recorded to date is **sufficient to mandate rejection of `SF-26-0708` and Lift 6**, and is **NOT sufficient to support acceptance of the Block A slab-on-grade fill pad** under Specification 31 20 00 due to three record and compliance deficiencies:
1. **Lift 6 Physical Non-Conformance and Invalid Reporting (`SF-26-0708`)**: Two of the four Lift 6 test locations (`FD-14` at Grid B-3 and `FD-17` at Grid G-2) failed the moisture window on the dry side ($\Delta w = -3.1\%$ and $-3.4\%$), and `SF-26-0708` reported against the prohibited ASTM D698 reference (`VMT-2024-0403-P1B`) without evaluating moisture compliance (`Articles 3.4.B, 3.4.C.3, 3.4.D, 3.7.B–C`).
2. **Missing Field Density Reports `FDR-BA-01` through `FDR-BA-05` for Lifts 1 through 5 (`Articles 1.5.B.1, 1.6.B, & 3.7.B–C`)**: Rowan Bay's placement log (`RB-FPR-BA-2026`, Cover row `R31`; Daily Placement Log rows `R19`–`R23` and `R31`; Note 6; Note 8) states that Lifts 1 through 5 (placed `06/22/2026`, `06/23/2026`, `06/25/2026`, `06/26/2026` [`2026-06-26`], and `06/30/2026` [`2026-06-30`] from El. `745.0 ft` to El. `748.0 ft`) were tested at `3` tests per lift (`15` tests total, covering `FD-01` through `FD-13` plus rework retests across reports **`FDR-BA-01`**, **`FDR-BA-02`**, **`FDR-BA-03`**, **`FDR-BA-04`**, and **`FDR-BA-05`**) and notes surface drying on `06/26/2026` and a `~1,500 sf` dry-side moisture rework pass near Grid E-4 on `06/30/2026` (Lift 5, row `R23`). However, **none of the underlying reports `FDR-BA-01` through `FDR-BA-05` are in the project verification record**. Given Vantree's use of the wrong Proctor and omission of moisture limits on `SF-26-0708`, Thelbridge cannot verify from the Contractor's summary log alone whether Lifts 1–5 met both $\gamma_d \ge 122.0\text{ pcf}$ and $8.2\% \le w \le 11.2\%$ against `VMT-25-0918-P04`.
3. **Unattached `RFI 079` Stockpile Gradation Retest (`Article 1.4.C`, `Article 3.7.A.2`, & `RFI 079` / `TPM-25-079`)**: `RFI 079` (closed `10/10/2025` via letter `TPM-25-079`) qualified the borrow stockpile north of Block C for reuse *"subject to gradation retest"*; that gradation retest confirming correspondence of the Block C stockpile to Sample `LWP-PA-SF-2025-01` (`VMT-25-0918-P04`) is not yet included in the verification record.
